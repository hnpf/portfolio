sudo is my shepherd 
i shall not segfault, as it maketh me lie down in root-owned directories 
it leadeth me beside symlinks of sanity 

and though i walk through the valley of missing packages i will fear no misconfig 
for sudo art with me thy password and thy prompt they comfort me 

thou preparest a shell before me in the presence of daemons 
thou anointest my \$PATH with binaries 

my stdout runneth over surely logs and dmesg shall follow me all the days of my uptime
i will dwell in the home of tux 
forever and ever 

sudo amen
